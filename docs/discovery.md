# Discovery notes

## 1. Market check

The digital wardrobe category is crowded. A 2026 comparison lists ten major apps: GetWardrobe, Acloset, Whering, Indyx, Fits, Alta, Clueless, Stylebook, Cladwell and Pureple. Whering reported 4 million downloads in its first three years (May 2024).

Most of these apps are built around outfit planning. Industry coverage names the same top complaint across the category: cataloguing an existing wardrobe takes too long, so users drop off.

**Opportunity:** focus on the buying moment rather than the dressing moment, and build the wardrobe gradually instead of in one upload session.

## 2. Survey

A 3-minute Google Form shared through Instagram stories and WhatsApp. Each question was tied to an assumption.

### Who answered (first 6 responses)
- Ages 20 to 35, mostly Bangalore
- Frequent shoppers: 2 buy weekly or more, 3 buy two to three times a month
- Everyone shops at malls and brand stores, often alongside Myntra or brand websites

### Findings

| Question | Result |
|---|---|
| Bought something similar to what you owned? | 4 often, 2 once or twice, 0 never |
| Forgot you owned something? | 4 often, 2 sometimes |
| Share of wardrobe worn regularly | 4 of 6 wear less than 25%; nobody wears more than half |
| Confidence about what you own while shopping (1 to 5) | Average 2.7. Frequent duplicate buyers scored 1 to 3; people with an organised cupboard scored 4 |
| How do you track your clothes? | Nobody uses a system or an app |
| "Snap in the shop, see similar items you own" (1 to 5) | 5/5 from 4 people, 3/5 from 2 |
| Would you photograph each item? | 2 easily, 1 if under 10 seconds, 3 probably not or no |

### Features people valued most (up to 3 picks each)

| Feature | Votes |
|---|---|
| Search by colour or type | 3 |
| See what I have not worn in months | 3 |
| Track spending on clothes | 3 |
| Check for similar items before buying | 2 |
| Reminders of forgotten items | 2 |

## 3. Interpretation

- **Problem: validated (directionally).** Duplicate buying and forgotten clothes are frequent.
- **Main risk: effort to add items.** Two people who rated the idea 5/5 still said they would not photograph their clothes. This is the gap between wanting the outcome and doing the work.
- **A contradiction to resolve.** Described alone, the pre-purchase check scored highest. Picked from a list, search and "not worn in months" scored higher. Interviews need to settle which one is the hero feature.

## 4. Limitations

- Six responses, mostly from my own network, so the sample is small and likely friendly.
- The open question ("tell me about your last duplicate buy") got almost no stories. Interviews are needed for the "why".

## 5. Decisions that came from this

| Finding | Design response |
|---|---|
| People will not spend time adding clothes | Auto colour detection, one-tap type, timer showing seconds taken |
| Search valued highly | Search on the home screen, plain-language queries |
| Online shopping is common | Check flow accepts shopping-app screenshots, not only camera photos |
| "Not worn in months" valued | Forgotten gems in Insights, wear logging on every item |
